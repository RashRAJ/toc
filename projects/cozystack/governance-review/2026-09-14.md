### Governance Summary

Cozystack is a platform and framework for building clouds on bare metal,
provisioning Kubernetes clusters, virtual machines, managed databases and load
balancers through a REST API. It is built on Talos Linux, Kubernetes, Flux and
KubeVirt.

Governance documents live in
[cozystack/cozystack](https://github.com/cozystack/cozystack):
[GOVERNANCE.md](https://github.com/cozystack/cozystack/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md),
[CONTRIBUTOR_LADDER.md](https://github.com/cozystack/cozystack/blob/main/CONTRIBUTOR_LADDER.md),
[MAINTAINERS.md](https://github.com/cozystack/cozystack/blob/main/MAINTAINERS.md),
[CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/cozystack/cozystack/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md),
[CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](https://github.com/cozystack/cozystack/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
and [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/cozystack/cozystack/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
Design proposals and meeting notes live in
[cozystack/community](https://github.com/cozystack/community). Development spans
roughly 45 repositories in the `cozystack` organisation, which GOVERNANCE.md
classifies by whether a repository's output reaches the artifact an adopter
installs.

The project is led by its maintainers in a flat
[maintainer council](https://contribute.cncf.io/projects/best-practices/governance/templates/governance-maintainer)
style, deciding by consensus and falling back to a simple majority. A four-rung
contributor ladder governs advancement, with the Reviewer rung delegating area
ownership through `CODEOWNERS`. MAINTAINERS.md lists 9 active maintainers across
3 organisations, 3 Reviewers and 4 Emeritus maintainers. A Director role carries
a vote but has never been filled.

### Governance Evolution

**Governance has continuously been iterated upon by the project as a result of
their experience applying it, with the governance history demonstrating
evolution of maturity alongside the project's maturity evolution.**
<br>
**Incubating:** Suggested | **Graduated:** Suggested

Governance docs began in Feb 2024 (CoC, CONTRIBUTING.md, MAINTAINERS.md), the repo moved out of the aenix-io namespace in Mar 2025 alongside Sandbox acceptance, GOVERNANCE.md followed in Apr 2025, CONTRIBUTOR_LADDER.md in Jul 2025, and SECURITY.md in Mar 2026. Two changes were driven by applying the governance: the ladder gained a concrete technical offboarding and reactivation procedure in May 2026, just before the project first exercised it on the Jul 2026 roster changes; and SECURITY.md was rewritten in Aug 2026 answering the General Technical Review, removing an invented backport rule and recording that none exists — self-correction under external review. Caveat: the maturation is back-loaded, with GOVERNANCE.md's sub-projects section and most of SECURITY.md dating to Aug 2026.

### Decisions and Role Assignments

**Document how the project makes decisions on leadership roles, contribution
acceptance, requests to the CNCF, and changes to governance or project goals.**
<br>
**Incubating:** Suggested | **Graduated:** Required

[GOVERNANCE.md](https://github.com/cozystack/cozystack/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md)
sets one rule — consensus of maintainers and directors, falling back to simple
majority — with role changes detailed in
[CONTRIBUTOR_LADDER.md](https://github.com/cozystack/cozystack/blob/main/CONTRIBUTOR_LADDER.md)
(nomination PR, two approvals for Reviewer, maintainer majority for Maintainer)
and contribution acceptance in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/cozystack/cozystack/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
plus [CODEOWNERS](https://github.com/cozystack/cozystack/blob/main/.github/CODEOWNERS).
Three gaps: GOVERNANCE.md and the ladder give conflicting routes to
maintainership; requests to the CNCF and governance amendments have no process of
their own, and no quorum or tie-break is defined; and Directors hold a vote but
none is named.

**Document how role, function-based members, or sub-teams are assigned,
onboarded, and removed for specific teams (example: Security Response
Committee).**
<br>
**Incubating:** Suggested | **Graduated:** Required

The Reviewer role is fully specified — area ownership in
[CODEOWNERS](https://github.com/cozystack/cozystack/blob/main/.github/CODEOWNERS),
entry by nomination PR with two approvals, exit via the inactivity and
involuntary-removal rules — and the
[technical offboarding](https://github.com/cozystack/cozystack/blob/main/CONTRIBUTOR_LADDER.md#stepping-downemeritus-process)
is unusually concrete, covering GitHub team removal, org role downgrade, and
credential rotation within five business days. Gaps:
[SECURITY.md](https://github.com/cozystack/cozystack/blob/main/SECURITY.md) names
five maintainers responsible for security response but no process for joining or
leaving that group; the `engineering` GitHub team granting write access appears
only in the offboarding steps, with membership undefined; and Directors are
electable but unfilled.

### Subprojects

**All subprojects, if any, are listed.**
<br>
**Incubating:** Required | **Graduated:** Required

The subprojects are listed in GOVERNANCE.md, but not in the incubation
application, and the two lists disagree materially.

[GOVERNANCE.md](https://github.com/cozystack/cozystack/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md#sub-projects)
partitions the organisation by whether a repository's output reaches the artifact
an adopter installs: 6 packaged sub-projects, 9 independent tools, 6
infrastructure repos, and forks. It also volunteers what most projects leave
buried, naming the fork compiled into the product, the four private repositories
and why each is private, and the telemetry server that adopters never install but
whose clusters report to it by default.

The application's list of nine is stale by comparison. It omits four packaged
sub-projects that ship code to adopters (keycloak-kms-proxy, local-ccm,
cozystack-scheduler, ingress-nginx-with-protobuf-exporter) plus the apimachinery
fork; lists `cozypkg`, renamed to `cozyhr`; lists `cozystack-ui`, archived in June
2026 and carrying no licence; and cites `aenix-io/etcd-operator`, which has been
donated to `cozystack/etcd-operator` and now resolves only by redirect. That last
item is the one place the application names a vendor-owned URL as project
property and should be corrected.

One correction against GOVERNANCE.md itself: it states that apart from
apimachinery, forks are not built into the distribution. That is not true of
[cozystack/kilo](https://github.com/cozystack/kilo), since
[packages/system/kilo/values.yaml](https://github.com/cozystack/cozystack/blob/main/packages/system/kilo/values.yaml)
pins `ghcr.io/cozystack/cozystack/kilo` by digest. Several active repositories are
also unlisted, including `kilo-clustermesh-operator`, `windows-golden-image` and
`homebrew-tap`.
